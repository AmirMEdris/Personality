04 / The engineering judgment

Every choice has a reason.

Preserve your idea

A questionnaire can connect to a model when its training measurements match. The LISS route preserves all 50 documented items; we do not fabricate a NEO conversion.

Keep outcomes interpretable

“Two substances in a month” describes a reported behavior. Recency is a time window, not a dose, addiction score or causal effect.

Make complexity earn its place

The stack combines complementary models. Selection happens before the test audit; a simpler candidate can win.

Make uncertainty visible

Show precision, recall, calibration, counts and intervals together. A persuasive interface cannot substitute for evidence.

The remaining dependency is matched, permitted data with enough positive examples. LISS access and model-release permission are not yet secured. No claim of personal drug-use prediction is made.
